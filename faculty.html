Dr. B. T. Muddesha

Director

Dr. B. T. Muddesha is an accomplished academician and media professional with nearly three decades of experience spanning journalism education and the print, television, and digital media industries. He has authored more than 20 books related to journalism education, making significant contributions to the field.

His extensive professional experience in the media industry has enriched his teaching and strengthened his academic expertise. From 1997 to 2007, he worked as a Senior Reporter and Sub-Editor in news production at Udaya Television, gaining valuable practical experience in television journalism and news production.

In September 2007, Dr. Muddesha joined SSCMS as an Assistant Professor, serving in that capacity until 2013. Subsequently, he assumed the responsibilities of Director of Media Studies and contributed to the development of Radio Siddhartha 90.8 FM.

He was awarded a Ph.D. by the University of Mysore for his research on the quality of language used in electronic media news, under the guidance of Professor Usharani.

As an academician, Dr. Muddesha actively participates in seminars, conferences, and workshops, demonstrating his continued commitment to academic excellence, professional development, and the advancement of journalism education.

Dr. Jyothi C.

Assistant Professor | Head of the Department | Placement Officer

Dr. Jyothi C. is an experienced academician in Journalism and Mass Communication, with over 16 years of teaching experience at Sri Siddhartha Centre for Media Studies (SSCMS). She currently serves as the Head of the Department of Journalism and Mass Communication and also holds the responsibility of Placement Officer, supporting students in identifying career opportunities and transitioning into the media industry.

She completed her postgraduate degree in M.S. Communication at Central College, Bangalore University. Before entering academia, she gained three years of professional experience in print journalism, working as a reporter for The Times of India and Eesanje. She joined SSCMS in 2010 and has since been actively involved in teaching, academic administration, research, and student mentoring. She qualified for the Karnataka State Eligibility Test (KSET) in 2014 and was awarded a Ph.D. in Journalism and Mass Communication by the University of Mysore in 2024.

Dr. Jyothi C. specialises in print journalism, with professional experience in reporting, news writing, and journalistic practices. Her industry experience enriches her teaching approach, enabling her to integrate practical journalistic skills with theoretical knowledge and prepare students for professional careers in media and communication.

Dr. Jyothi C. has presented more than ten research papers at national and international conferences. She has published research papers in UGC-CARE-listed journals and Scopus-indexed publications, contributing to scholarly discussions in Journalism and Mass Communication. She has also participated in more than 40 Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs), conducted in both online and offline modes, reflecting her commitment to continuous professional development and academic excellence.

As an educator, Dr. Jyothi C. actively guides students in dissertation research and practical journal production. She mentors students in the preparation of Siddhartha Sampada, a practical training journal that helps them develop reporting, writing, editing, and publication skills. Her academic approach emphasises experiential learning, critical thinking, research aptitude, and professional readiness. Through her responsibilities as Head of the Department and Placement Officer, she contributes to departmental development and supports students in preparing for employment opportunities in the media industry.

Dr. Jyothi C. has contributed to radio broadcasting through talks aired on All India Radio (AIR). She has also conducted interviews and produced radio programmes broadcast on Radio Siddhartha 90.8 FM. These activities reflect her engagement with broadcast media and her commitment to connecting academic learning with professional media practices. Through her combined experience in journalism, teaching, research, academic leadership, and media production, Dr. Jyothi C. continues to contribute to the advancement of journalism education and the professional development of aspiring media practitioners.

Mrs. Swetha M. P.

Assistant Professor

Mrs. Swetha M. P. is an experienced academician who has been serving as an Assistant Professor at the institution for 17 years. Her academic background, professional training, research contributions, and teaching experience reflect her commitment to journalism and media education.

She has qualified for the Karnataka State Eligibility Test (KSET) in 2014, conducted through the University of Mysore. She completed the Vani Certificate Course offered by All India Radio, Bengaluru, and secured the top position in the M.Sc. Electronic Media programme at Tumkur University in 2007. She also gained practical experience through an internship at Doordarshan, Bengaluru.

Her research interests are reflected in her presentation of more than ten research papers at national and international conferences. She has actively participated in numerous offline and online seminars and conferences and completed a refresher course offered by Kurukshetra University through online mode.

In addition to her teaching and research activities, Mrs. Swetha M. P. has served as a Board of Examinations member for the Departments of Journalism and Mass Communication at Tumkur University and Gulbarga University. She has also been invited as a resource person to deliver lectures at Akkamahadevi University, Vijayapura, and Kannada University, Hampi, on several occasions.

Her teaching expertise encompasses a range of subjects in journalism, media production, and communication studies, including Radio Programme Production, Audio-Visual Production, Film Communication, Communication for Development, Communication Theories, and Television Anchoring Skills.

She has submitted her doctoral thesis to the University of Mysore and is currently awaiting her final Ph.D. viva voce examination. Her continued engagement in teaching, research, academic collaboration, and professional development demonstrates her dedication to advancing journalism and media education.

Kokila M. S.

Assistant Professor

Ms. Kokila M. S. is an experienced media educator, journalist, researcher, and media production professional with over a decade of experience in teaching Journalism and Mass Communication. With an academic background in Electronic Media and a K-SET qualification, she has contributed to higher education, print and television journalism, public relations, documentary production, and community radio. Her professional expertise encompasses journalism, audio-visual production, voice-over artistry, media writing, and strategic communication.

Ms. Kokila M. S. has over a decade of teaching experience in Journalism and Mass Communication. She has served for five years at the University College of Arts, Tumkur University; five years as a Lecturer at Sri Siddhartha Centre for Media Studies, Tumkur; and one year in the Postgraduate Department of Journalism and Mass Communication at Tumkur University.

Her teaching experience enables her to integrate theoretical knowledge with practical media training, helping students develop journalistic skills, media production capabilities, critical thinking, and professional competence. She is committed to academic excellence and to preparing students for the evolving demands of the media industry.

Her professional experience spans print journalism, television reporting, public relations, documentary production, community radio, and voice-over artistry. She worked as a Reporter at Janasri News for one year, covering live reports, walkthroughs, chit-chats, and routine news packages. She also served as a Public Relations Officer at MJS PR, Bengaluru, gaining practical experience in public relations and professional communication.

As a columnist for the Kannada daily Vijayavani, under the Vidyarthi Mithra column, she contributed educational lessons for various subjects. Her articles and writings have also appeared in leading newspapers and magazines, including Prajavani, Vijaya Karnataka, Vijaya Next, Samyukta Karnataka, Kannada Prabha, and Sakhi.

In the field of media production, she has produced documentaries for Doordarshan and radio programmes for Radio Siddhartha 90.8 FM. Her work as a voice-over artist includes contributions to documentaries and local advertisements. These diverse professional engagements have strengthened her practical understanding of journalism, media production, public relations, and strategic communication.

Ms. Kokila M. S. has presented research papers at seven national conferences, contributing to academic discussions in Journalism and Mass Communication. Her academic interests include media analysis, journalism practice, academic writing, and audio-visual storytelling. Through research presentations and scholarly engagement, she continues to contribute to the development of media education and communication studies.

Ms. Kokila M. S. is committed to fostering students' theoretical understanding, practical skills, creativity, and critical thinking. By combining academic knowledge with professional media experience, she strives to equip students with the competencies required to navigate the dynamic and evolving landscape of journalism and mass communication.

Lecturer & Filmmaker Connect on LinkedIn

Naveen N G

Lecturer / Independent Filmmaker

Mr. Naveen is an experienced academician and independent filmmaker with a strong background in Mass Communication, Media Studies, and Film Production. He has been associated with SSCMS as a Lecturer for the past ten years, contributing to academic teaching, student mentoring, and curriculum-related activities in the field of media and communication studies. His academic engagement focuses on developing students' theoretical understanding and practical skills in media, journalism, and communication.

Alongside his academic career, Mr. Naveen is actively involved in independent filmmaking, directing and producing films with a focus on creative storytelling, visual communication, and cinematic expression. His filmmaking practice reflects his interest in exploring creative narratives and translating ideas into meaningful visual experiences.

He also brings professional experience from the television broadcasting industry. He worked as at Pragathi TV, Tumkur for one year, where he was involved in media editing, content curation, and television broadcast workflows. During this tenure, he contributed to the development and delivery of engaging media content.

Prior to joining SSCMS, he served as a Lecturer in Mass Communication at Akka Mahadevi Women's University, Vijayapura, from 2013 to 2015. In this role, he delivered lectures in Mass Communication and guided students in developing theoretical knowledge and practical competencies in media and communication.

His professional journey also includes an internship at Suvarna News in 2012, where he gained practical exposure to television journalism, newsroom operations, and broadcast media production.

With a combination of academic experience, industry exposure, and creative filmmaking practice, Mr. Naveen brings an integrated perspective to media education, encouraging students to connect classroom learning with professional media practices.

Who We Are

About Sri Siddhartha

Empowering communication skills and modern visual training nestled in a magnificent green eco-system.

SSCMS - Dynamic Educational Incubator

Situated in the 65-acre clean, idyllic campus of SSIT, Sri Siddhartha Centre for Media Studies has carved a unique niche for itself in media education and analytical communications research. SSCMS is housed inside a spacious custom-designed building, surrounded by lush lawns, ornamental trees, and paved pathing.

With proximity to Bangalore, the IT & broadcasting hotspot, our students enjoy exclusive advantages regarding high-profile field internships, frequent industry visits, and top-tier studio placements. Learning is fully participative, utilizing active workshops, production of physical lab journals, and interactive group-dialogue seminars.

"SSCMS represents the right educational space to become a seasoned, highly creative media expert ready to confront modern challenges."

Our Vision

Modern communication has largely shaped the global village, awareness and understanding has gone global. Media communication is an effective method to generate peace on earth through awareness, understanding and thereby establishing harmony. It is the endeavor of SSCMS to take the first step towards this ultimate ideal.

Our Mission

Advances in media, technology and society have posed several challenges in our contemporary times in terms of social inequality, freedom of expression, democratization of information and invasion into the privacy of individuals. SSCMS aims at establishing a meaningful role in seeking solutions to these issues through participative teaching, specialized training and exhaustive research in the field of media.
